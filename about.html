About

A little about me

I'm Aadish — a solutions architect and database engineer based in Bengaluru, India. I design and build business solutions end-to-end: from database architecture and Oracle APEX applications to systems that tie it all together.

I'm technology-agnostic by principle, and that now includes how I build: pure hand-written code, AI-assisted development, or a mix of both — whichever gets a robust solution into production fastest. The focus is less on any one stack and more on architecting systems that solve real business problems.

Outside of work, I like exploring new technologies, writing occasionally about what I learn, and traveling when I get the chance.
