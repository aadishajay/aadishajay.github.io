Ansible , you beauty!

Published by Aadish

Another way of scripting and much more

Automation is fast happening , and so is the workload of infra guys. At the start of my career I had seen experienced infra personnel getting worried if they would lose job if cloud takes over. And now, over a decade ahead I could see most of them are already working on cloud and their workload is multi-fold compared to when they were on on-premise.

What has happened with the cloud adoption is that the older single , large server was replaced with numerous , specialised and smaller virtual machines in the cloud. Now maintaining these huge number of similar piece of infrastructure is not an easy task. The guys who were worried of finding job were then worried on the amount of work they are to take now. Ironic , isn’t it.

In many cases of maintenance , same commands need to be executed over multiple infrastructure. Traditional way of doing manually , one by one or by keeping multiple window open in parallel all were bound to hit one or the other issues , including human errors.

The came Ansible , a tool crafted specifically for this case of running same commands on multiple machines. The architecture is quite simple , doesn’t need anything else other than python being available in all the target machines and ssh connectivity being established. It does a very clean job and leaves not much of a mess behind.

This note is not supposed to be guide or a hand holding article for Ansible. I would say just explore it.

Start off with installing Ansible :

python -m pip install --user ansible

And then try out some cool stuff.
