1d:T98b,<p>In this age of increased adoption of cloud and micro-services , we often stumble upon situations where-in a large number of VMs are to be set-up . Handling them in the classical way is an impossible task.</p>
<p>Imagine the situation of need to maintain a servers in the scale of hundreds on the cloud. The huge number causes these problems</p>
<ul>
<li>Provisioning such infrastructure at scale</li>
<li>Maintaining such an inventory when operational</li>
<li>Decommissioning them when they are no more required</li>
</ul>
<p>It would be quite cumbersome , especially maintaining them.
<img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1597115964035-f49f2b555c85?crop=entropy&#x26;cs=tinysrgb&#x26;fm=jpg&#x26;ixlib=rb-1.2.1&#x26;q=80&#x26;raw_url=true&#x26;ixid=MnwxMjA3fDB8MHxwaG90by1wYWdlfHx8fGVufDB8fHx8&#x26;auto=format&#x26;fit=crop&#x26;w=1548" alt=""></p>
<blockquote>
<p>Photo by [Acton Crawford]</p>
</blockquote>
<p>This a one of the many situations where Infrastructure as a Code (IaaC) comes into play. Think about being able to do all the three steps mentioned above through code. Terraform does exactly that. The code in-fact are configurations file written in Hashicorp Configuration Language (HCL) , or optionally JSON. 
But , the coolest feature I have felt is the configuration files act as the inventory maintainer , ie when you run the same set of codes , it will apply only the changes since the last run.</p>
<p>Personally , I had felt Terraform like a magic , that helps to being in automation to yet another domain - provisioning. You can just create any number of VMs on the cloud , and upscale or downscale them , add various attributes and on a later date if you wish , even terminate the infrastructure.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><a href="https://youtu.be/h970ZBgKINg">Watch this video with detailed explanation</a></p>
</blockquote>
<p>Terraform again is well equipped with testing and verification mechanism via its various commands like init , validate , plan , apply and destroy.</p>
<p>Once you start exploring terraform you will find that its no less than magic.
So , as always , keep exploring.</p>
