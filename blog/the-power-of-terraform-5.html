The Power of Terraform

Published by Aadish

Explore the magic of Infrasctructure as a Code (IaaC) tool called Terraform. This tool can provision , maintain and even decommission infrastructure on cloud.

In this age of increased adoption of cloud and micro-services , we often stumble upon situations where-in a large number of VMs are to be set-up . Handling them in the classical way is an impossible task.

Imagine the situation of need to maintain a servers in the scale of hundreds on the cloud. The huge number causes these problems

  • Provisioning such infrastructure at scale
  • Maintaining such an inventory when operational
  • Decommissioning them when they are no more required

It would be quite cumbersome , especially maintaining them.

Photo by [Acton Crawford]

This a one of the many situations where Infrastructure as a Code (IaaC) comes into play. Think about being able to do all the three steps mentioned above through code. Terraform does exactly that. The code in-fact are configurations file written in Hashicorp Configuration Language (HCL) , or optionally JSON.  But , the coolest feature I have felt is the configuration files act as the inventory maintainer , ie when you run the same set of codes , it will apply only the changes since the last run.

Personally , I had felt Terraform like a magic , that helps to being in automation to yet another domain - provisioning. You can just create any number of VMs on the cloud , and upscale or downscale them , add various attributes and on a later date if you wish , even terminate the infrastructure.

Watch this video with detailed explanation

Terraform again is well equipped with testing and verification mechanism via its various commands like init , validate , plan , apply and destroy.

Once you start exploring terraform you will find that its no less than magic. So , as always , keep exploring.
